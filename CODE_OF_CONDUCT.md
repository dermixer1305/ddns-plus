# Code of Conduct

DDNS+ aims to be a practical and respectful open-source project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction.
- Provide actionable feedback when reviewing issues or pull requests.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Publishing private information without permission
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the project or its contributors.
